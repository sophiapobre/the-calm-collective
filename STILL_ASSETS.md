## Photography Guidelines

### Visual Style
- **Lighting:** Natural, soft, diffused — avoid harsh shadows
- **Background:** Neutral (ivory, beige, gray), textured (linen, wood, stone)
- **Composition:** Minimal, clean, generous negative space
- **Props:** Natural materials (ceramic, wood, stone, linen)
- **Mood:** Calm, warm, inviting, authentic

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1500x1500px for products
- **Format:** JPG or PNG with transparency
- **Color profile:** sRGB
- **File size:** Optimized for web (under 500KB)

### Image Dimensions
- **Product Card:** 500x500px (1:1 square)
- **Product Detail:** 1000x1000px (1:1 square)
- **Hero Banner:** 1920x800px (wide format)
- **Category Banner:** 1200x400px (3:1 ratio)

### File Naming Convention
```
<category>-<product-name>-<variant>.jpg

Examples:
tea-cloud-mist-green-tea-loose-50g.jpg
candles-driftwood-amber-medium.jpg
journals-still-journal-linen-ivory.jpg
home-ceramic-mug-set-natural.jpg
body-botanic-bath-soak-lavender.jpg
```

## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Tone
- **Calm** — Never rushed, never urgent
- **Authentic** — Honest about materials, process, origins
- **Inviting** — Warm but not overly friendly
- **Intentional** — Every word chosen carefully

### Language Style
- Short, clear sentences
- Focus on sensory details (texture, scent, light)
- Avoid hype or exaggeration
- Use natural, grounding imagery

### What to Say ✅
- "Slow down and savor every sip"
- "Made from natural linen that softens with time"
- "A moment of stillness in your day"
- "Handcrafted with intention"

### What to Avoid ❌
- "AMAZING deal!!!"
- "You NEED this!"
- "Limited time only!"
- Over-the-top enthusiasm

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Download Product Images
1. Visit Unsplash/Pexels
2. Search for: "ceramic tea cup", "linen journal", "natural candle", "stone tray", "handmade soap"
3. Download high-resolution images (1500px+)
4. Rename files using naming convention
5. Save to `backend/public/images/`

### Step 2: Update Categories in Database
Create these categories:
- Tea & Beverage
- Candles & Scents
- Journals & Stationery
- Home & Ritual
- Body & Care

### Step 3: Add Products
Use the product descriptions above as templates. For each product include:
- Name
- Description (2-3 sentences, sensory and calming)
- Price(s)
- Variants (size, color, material, scent)
- Category
- Image path
- Tags

### Step 4: Test & Refine
- Check that all images display correctly
- Verify product descriptions match the calm, minimal tone
- Test variant selection and pricing
- Ensure checkout flow works smoothly
